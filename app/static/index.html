How the series is harmonized

Hotspots from NASA FIRMS (MODIS C6.1 Terra/Aqua, VIIRS 375 m S-NPP and NOAA-20) are filtered (MODIS confidence ≥ 30, VIIRS nominal/high, static industrial sources removed), counted on a 0.25° grid per month, and MODIS counts are converted to VIIRS S-NPP-equivalent counts with a model calibrated where both sensors overlap.

Three models were compared with year-blocked cross-validation (train 2012–2018, test 2019–2025). The lowest error model is used for 2001–2011.

Data: NASA FIRMS (MODIS C6.1 MCD14, VIIRS 375 m VNP14IMG / VJ114IMG). Boundaries: Natural Earth. Counts are satellite hotspot detections, not the number of fires or burned area.
