# Demo video script (30 seconds)

| Time | On screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 s | App opens on Kazakhstan; burn calendar fills in | "Two NASA satellites, two fire records that don't match. Burn Calendar makes them one." |
| 4–9 s | Before/after chart: raw MODIS and VIIRS lines, then the single harmonized line | "We converted 25 years of MODIS hotspots into VIIRS-equivalent counts, validated on 2019 to 2025." |
| 9–15 s | Draw a polygon over Kostanay region on the map; calendar, map points and KPIs update | "Draw any area, and you get its own fire calendar: when it burns, and the individual hotspots." |
| 15–21 s | Pick "Month to assess" = October 2026; anomaly card and highlighted bars | "Is this month abnormal? Every month is compared with its 25-year median and range." |
| 21–26 s | Annual chart with trend line; KPI "−7 % per year" | "And the long-term trend: fire activity in Kazakhstan has fallen by seven percent a year." |
| 26–30 s | Click "Points CSV", camera icon on a chart; end card with URL and NASA FIRMS credit | "Download it all. Burn Calendar, built on NASA FIRMS." |

Recording tips: 1920×1080 browser window at 90 % zoom; pre-load the page once so the map tiles are
cached; keep the cursor slow and deliberate; add the live URL as on-screen text in the last 4 seconds.
